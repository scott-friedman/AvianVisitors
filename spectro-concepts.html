Spectral Concepts

Four ways to show a bird's call as a picture, inside the info card. All four are driven by the same FFT the live site already computes from each recording — so any of them is a re-skin, not new plumbing. Calls here are synthesized so the page runs offline; on the real site the actual clip slots in unchanged. Pick a bird, then hit play on any tile (or play all).
